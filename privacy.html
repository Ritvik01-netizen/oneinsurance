Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 19 September 2026

This is a draft, not a final legal document. It describes, in plain language, what data OneInsurance actually collects and why, based on how the site currently works. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as a final, binding policy until that review is complete.

What we collect

When you get a quote or apply for a policy, we collect the details needed to process that application: your name, contact details (email, phone), date of birth, address, and — where the insurer requires it for underwriting — a national ID or passport number. We only ask for identity document numbers when the specific product needs them, not by default.

Guest checkout

You can request a quote and submit an application without creating an account. If you do, we store the same minimum contact and application details as we would for a registered customer, associated with the application itself rather than a user profile. You can create an account afterwards to see and manage that application going forward.

How we use it

  • To process your insurance application with the insurer you selected.
  • To contact you about that application's status (by email and/or WhatsApp).
  • To send renewal reminders for policies you hold with us.
  • To let our admin/broker team review and process applications.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Where it's stored

Application and account data is stored with our backend provider (Google Firebase, hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure). Access is restricted to accounts flagged as administrators on our team.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to what we're required to retain for regulatory or record-keeping purposes once a policy is active. Contact us via WhatsApp or email to make a request.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details on the limited local storage we use to keep you signed in and remember your language/theme preference.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes materially, and update the "last updated" date above.
