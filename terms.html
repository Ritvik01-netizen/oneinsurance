Terms of Use

Draft — last updated 19 September 2026

This is a draft, not a final legal document. It describes, in plain language, how OneInsurance currently works. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as a final, binding contract until that review is complete.

What OneInsurance is

OneInsurance is a comparison platform for insurance products offered by our appointed insurers in Mauritius. We compare plans, help you apply, and pass your application to the relevant insurer. We are not the insurer — the insurer underwrites your policy and pays out on any claim.

Submitting an application

Requesting a quote or completing checkout submits an application, not an active policy. An application is reviewed and confirmed by our team before it becomes active. We'll tell you clearly which status your application is in, and it never becomes an active policy automatically just because it was submitted.

Guest checkout

You may apply without creating an account. Your application is still processed and stored the same way as a registered customer's. You can create an account at any time afterwards, using the same contact details, to see and manage it going forward.

Payments

We currently accept Juice by MCB, Blink by Emtel, MyT Money and bank transfer. We don't yet process card payments through an automated gateway — payments are confirmed manually by our team once received, and your application status updates once that's done.

Pricing shown on the site

Prices, "you could save" figures, and similar numbers on this site are illustrative unless otherwise stated, and are not a binding quote. Your actual premium is confirmed by the insurer during underwriting.

Cancellation / cooling-off

Standard consumer cooling-off terms apply as set by the underwriting insurer for your product — check your policy documents once issued for the specific period and process.

Changes to these terms

We'll update this page if our terms change materially, and update the "last updated" date above.
