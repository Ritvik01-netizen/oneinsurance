Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Is OneInsurance really free to use? ▼
Yes — 100% free, forever. We earn a commission from insurers when you buy a policy, so you never pay us anything. The price you see is the same or better than going direct.
How do you compare prices from all insurers? ▼
Our aggregator engine connects to insurer pricing systems and fetches live quotes based on your details. All results are normalised so you compare like-for-like coverage — not just headline prices. Each plan also gets a Coverage Score out of 100.
Is my cyclone & flood damage covered? ▼
Every home & motor plan we show clearly labels cyclone and flood cover. Use the "Cyclone cover" filter on the results page, and check the Moris Risk Map to understand your district's exposure. Essential for Mauritius — especially Nov–May.
How do I pay? Can I use Juice, Blink or MyT Money? ▼
Yes! We accept Visa/Mastercard, bank transfer, Juice by MCB, Blink by Emtel and MyT Money. All payments are 256-bit SSL encrypted and processed securely.
What happens when my policy expires? ▼
We send renewal reminders 30 days before expiry by SMS, email and WhatsApp — and automatically re-compare the whole market so you always renew at the best available price. No lapses, no surprises.
What is a no-claims bonus (NCB)? ▼
Every year you drive without claiming, your insurer gives you a discount at renewal — up to 60% in Mauritius after several claim-free years. When you switch insurers through us, your NCB follows you. We show NCB protection options on every motor quote.

All clearNo warning
Class I–II36–12h before gusts

Your pre-season checklist

✓Check your home policy includes cyclone & flood cover (not all do!)
✓Photograph your home, roof & contents — timestamped proof speeds up claims
✓Confirm your car is comprehensive, not just third party (cyclone damage isn't covered on TP)
✓Save your insurer's 24/7 claims hotline in your phone
✓Keep policy documents in cloud storage, not just paper

After a cyclone

Report damage within 7 days. File through our Claims Centre with photos — cyclone claims are fast-tracked and most insurers waive excess if your plan includes "zero excess on cyclone". Class III/IV events trigger our priority claims line.

At the scene — do this in order

✓Stop safely, hazard lights on. Check everyone is OK — call 114 if anyone is hurt
✓Injuries or dispute? Call Police 999 and don't move the vehicles
✓Photograph everything: positions, damage, number plates, road marks, the other driver's vignette
✓Exchange details: name, NID, phone, insurer + policy number (show your Digital Vignette)
✓Minor accident, no injuries, both agree? Fill an agreed statement (constat à l'amiable) together
✓Report to your insurer within 24–48h — or file right here in the Claims Centre
