Cookie Policy

Draft — last updated 19 September 2026

This is a draft, not a final legal document. It describes what this site actually stores in your browser today. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

What we store

OneInsurance uses your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking or advertising cookies) for a small number of things that make the site work:

WhatWhy
Sign-in sessionKeeps you signed in between visits, via Firebase Authentication.
Language preferenceRemembers whether you chose English, Français or Kreol Morisien.
Theme preferenceRemembers light/dark mode.

What we don't do

We don't use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. We don't sell browsing data.

Managing storage

You can clear this data at any time through your browser's settings, which will sign you out and reset your saved preferences.
